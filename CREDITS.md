# Credits

Third-party assets used in 6/7, with attribution.

Attribution is given for every asset regardless of whether its licence
requires it. CC0 imposes no obligation to credit; crediting anyway is simply
the right thing to do, and it keeps the provenance of every file in the repo
answerable.
